# B.Sc. Nursing Standardized OSCE Procedure Checklist Library
## Standardized Clinical Skills & Evaluation Rubrics
*Compliant with Indian Nursing Council (INC) Competency Curriculum, WHO Guidelines, Mission Niramaya (UPSMF/Jhpiego), and TNNMC*

---

## Evaluation Scale Legend
Each step is evaluated on the standard 3-point clinical competency scale:
*   **[2] Competent / Done Completely**: Performed accurately, following all aseptic, safety, and scientific principles without omission.
*   **[1] Partially Competent / Partially Done**: Attempted, but with minor omissions, minor hesitation, or non-hazardous procedural deviations.
*   **[0] Incompetent / Not Done**: Omitted completely, executed incorrectly, or violated core patient safety or infection control principles.
*   **[🚩 RED FLAG] Critical Safety Violation**: Direct breach of sterile technique in invasive procedure, wrong medication/dosage, wrong patient, or safety hazard.

### Procedure NF-01: Adult Intramuscular (IM) Injection (Ventrogluteal Site)
*Time Allotted: 5 Minutes | Maximum Score: 20 Marks*

```
STATION SETUP:
- Adult injection arm/hip simulator or Ventrogluteal manikin
- Sterile syringes (2ml / 3ml), needles (21G, 22G, 23G)
- Medication ampoule / vial (labeled distilled water), alcohol swabs, sterile dry cotton
- Kidney tray, pair of clean gloves, sterile gauze piece
- Sharp container (White puncture-proof) and BMW disposal bin (Yellow)
- Doctor's prescription chart and medication administration record (MAR)
```

| Step # | Procedure Steps | Max | Criteria & Scientific Rationale |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Phase 1: Pre-Procedure & Preparation** | | | |
| 1 | Verified doctor's prescription order; cross-checked 6 Rights (Right Patient, Drug, Dose, Route, Time, Documentation). | 2 | Prevents medication errors; legal safety. |
| 2 | Identified patient by checking ID band and asking name/hospital ID; verified allergy history. | 1 | Ensures positive patient identification. |
| 3 | Explained indication, action, and sensations of injection to patient; obtained informed consent. | 1 | Relieves anxiety and promotes cooperation. |
| 4 | Performed WHO 6-step hand hygiene; donned clean disposable gloves. | 2 | Reduces transmission of microorganisms. |
| 5 | Assembled equipment ergonomically on clean kidney tray; inspected ampoule expiry date, integrity, and clarity. | 1 | Ensures readiness and sterile integrity. |
| **Phase 2: Procedure Execution** | | | |
| 6 | Tapped top of ampoule, wrapped sterile gauze around neck, snapped neck away from body safely. | 1 | Prevents cut injury and glass contamination. |
| 7 | Inserted needle without touching outer rim; aspirated prescribed volume; expelled air bubbles at eye level. | 2 | Ensures exact dosage measurement. |
| 8 | Changed drawing needle to a fresh sterile intramuscular needle (21G or 22G, 1.5 inch). | 1 | Sharp needle minimizes tissue trauma and pain. |
| 9 | Positioned patient in side-lying (Sims) position with knees flexed to relax gluteal muscles. | 1 | Relaxes muscle for easier penetration. |
| 10 | Accurately located Ventrogluteal landmark: Placed palm over greater trochanter, index finger pointing toward anterior superior iliac spine (ASIS), middle finger spread toward iliac crest forming a 'V'. Injection site is the center of the 'V'. | 2 | Safest site; deep muscle, away from sciatic nerve and major vessels. |
| 11 | Cleaned injection site with 70% alcohol swab in circular motion from center outward (2 inches); allowed to air-dry completely (30 sec). | 1 | Prevents stinging and disinfects skin properly. |
| 12 | Retracted skin taut (or performed Z-track technique); inserted needle smoothly at 90° angle with quick dart-like motion. | 2 | Ensures deep intramuscular penetration. |
| 13 | Stabilized barrel with non-dominant hand; aspirated plunger for 5-10 seconds to check for blood return. | 1 | Verifies needle is not in a blood vessel. |
| 14 | Injected medication slowly and smoothly (approx. 10 sec/mL); waited 5-10 seconds before withdrawal. | 1 | Promotes even muscle absorption, reduces pain. |
| 15 | Withdrew needle smoothly along angle of insertion while applying gentle pressure with sterile dry cotton (no rubbing/massaging). | 1 | Prevents hematoma and tissue irritation. |
| **Phase 3: Post-Procedure & Safety** | | | |
| 16 | Discarded uncapped needle and syringe directly into White puncture-proof container; discarded swab in Yellow bag. | 1 | Prevents needle-stick injuries (BMW Rules 2016). |
| 17 | Repositioned patient comfortably; removed gloves; performed hand hygiene. | 1 | Promotes patient comfort and infection control. |
| 18 | Accurately recorded drug, dosage, site, date, time, and signed the MAR. | 1 | Legal and clinical continuity record. |

### Procedure NF-02: Female Indwelling Urinary Catheterization (Foley Catheter)
*Time Allotted: 7 Minutes | Maximum Score: 20 Marks*

```
STATION SETUP:
- Female catheterization manikin/pelvis
- Sterile Foley catheter (Size 14/16 Fr), sterile drainage bag
- Sterile catheterization tray: drape, fenestrated drape, sponge forceps, cotton balls, kidney tray, gallipot
- 2% Lignocaine jelly / sterile water-soluble lubricant, 10ml syringe with sterile distilled water
- Povidone-iodine solution (or sterile normal saline), sterile gloves (2 pairs), adhesive tape
- Yellow BMW bin, White container, disposable underpad (Mackintosh)
```

| Step # | Procedure Steps | Max | Criteria & Scientific Rationale |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| 1 | Verified physician order; verified patient ID with 2 identifiers; explained procedure; secured informed consent. | 1 | Communication and consent standards. |
| 2 | Maintained privacy with screens; placed waterproof Mackintosh under buttocks; arranged good lighting. | 1 | Patient dignity and procedural visibility. |
| 3 | Performed medical hand hygiene; positioned patient in dorsal recumbent position with knees flexed and hips rotated outwards. | 1 | Optimal anatomical visualization. |
| 4 | Performed initial perineal hygiene with soap and water if needed; removed unsterile gloves; performed hand wash. | 1 | Reduces baseline bacterial burden. |
| 5 | Opened sterile catheter pack using aseptic technique; created sterile field on bedside table. | 1 | Prevents contamination of sterile items. |
| 6 | Donned sterile gloves maintaining strict aseptic technique (closed or open gloving without touching outer surface). | 2 | Prevents introducing microorganisms into bladder. 🚩 **Red Flag: Breaching sterility** |
| 7 | Placed sterile fenestrated drape over perineum exposing only the vulva. | 1 | Enlarges sterile working field. |
| 8 | Tested Foley catheter balloon with sterile water; lubricated catheter tip (1-2 inches) with water-soluble lubricant. | 1 | Ensures balloon integrity and smooth insertion. |
| 9 | Using non-dominant hand, carefully retracted labia minora to expose urethral meatus. Maintained retraction until catheter was in place. | 2 | Non-dominant hand is now contaminated; retraction must NOT be released to prevent meatus recontamination. |
| 10 | Using dominant sterile hand and forceps with antiseptic-soaked swabs, cleansed meatus with downward strokes (far labium, near labium, directly down meatus; single swab per stroke). | 2 | Clean-to-dirty principle prevents transferring pathogens into urethra. |
| 11 | Held lubricated catheter 2-3 inches from tip; gently inserted into meatus until urine flowed (approx. 2-3 inches). | 2 | Identifies successful bladder entry. |
| 12 | Advanced catheter an additional 1 to 2 inches beyond urine flow point before balloon inflation. | 2 | Guarantees balloon is fully in the bladder, avoiding urethral rupture. |
| 13 | Inflated balloon slowly with prescribed volume (10 mL) of sterile water; pulled back gently until resistance was felt. | 1 | Anchors catheter securely at bladder neck. |
| 14 | Connected catheter to sterile drainage bag using aseptic connection (if not pre-connected). | 1 | Maintains closed sterile drainage system. |
| 15 | Secured catheter to inner thigh with tape leaving slight slack; positioned drainage bag below bladder level on bed frame. | 1 | Prevents tension on urethra and prevents urine reflux. |
| 16 | Disposed contaminated swabs and gloves into Yellow BMW bin; performed hand hygiene; recorded catheter size, balloon volume, urine output, and time. | 1 | Documentation and waste management compliance. |

### Procedure NF-03: Measurement of Blood Pressure (Auscultatory Method)
*Time Allotted: 5 Minutes | Maximum Score: 10 Marks*

| Step # | Procedure Steps | Max | Criteria & Scientific Rationale |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| 1 | Checked patient identity, explained procedure; ensured patient rested seated or supine for at least 5 minutes; checked patient did not consume caffeine/tobacco in last 30 min. | 1 | Ensures physiological baseline. |
| 2 | Positioned arm supported at heart level, palm facing upwards; exposed upper arm completely (no tight rolled sleeves). | 1 | Prevents hydrostatic pressure bias and venous congestion. |
| 3 | Selected correct cuff size (bladder width = 40% of arm circumference, length = 80-100% of arm circumference). | 1 | Inappropriate cuff size causes inaccurate readings (small cuff overestimates BP). |
| 4 | Palpated brachial artery; applied deflated cuff smoothly and snugly 2.5 cm (1 inch) above the antecubital fossa with artery marker centered over artery. | 1 | Ensures even pressure transmission. |
| 5 | Palpated radial pulse; inflated cuff rapidly until radial pulse disappeared; noted reading and inflated 30 mmHg above this point (palpatory estimation of systolic pressure). | 2 | Prevents underestimation due to auscultatory gap. |
| 6 | Deflated cuff completely; waited 30 seconds; placed cleaned diaphragm of stethoscope gently over brachial artery pulse (avoided tucking under cuff). | 1 | Tucking diaphragm under cuff creates artifact sounds. |
| 7 | Re-inflated cuff to 30 mmHg above palpated systolic pressure; deflated slowly at a steady rate of 2 to 3 mmHg per second. | 1 | Proper deflation rate ensures accurate sound detection. |
| 8 | Identified Phase I Korotkoff sound (first clear tapping sound) as Systolic BP; identified Phase V (complete disappearance of sounds) as Diastolic BP. | 1 | Standard AHA/WHO criteria for BP identification. |
| 9 | Deflated cuff rapidly and completely; removed cuff; cleaned earpieces and diaphragm with alcohol swab. | 1 | Restores circulation and ensures equipment hygiene. |
| 10 | Ingested and recorded exact systolic and diastolic values in mmHg (even numbers), patient position, and arm used; informed patient. | 1 | Clinical documentation and communication. |

### Procedure AHN-01: Adult Basic Life Support (BCLS / CPR)
*Time Allotted: 5 Minutes | Maximum Score: 20 Marks*

```
STATION SETUP:
- Adult BLS full-body CPR manikin with feedback monitor / clicker
- Bag-Valve-Mask (Ambu bag) with reservoir and adult mask
- Automated External Defibrillator (AED) training simulator with adult pads
- Pair of gloves, pocket mask, rigid hardboard under manikin
```

| Step # | Procedure Steps | Max | Criteria & Scientific Rationale |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| 1 | Assessed scene safety (ensured environment safe for rescuer and victim). | 1 | Rescuer safety first. |
| 2 | Checked for responsiveness: Tapped victim's shoulders firmly and shouted "Are you okay?". | 1 | Verifies unresponsiveness. |
| 3 | Called for immediate emergency medical help ("Call hospital Code Blue / 108; bring emergency crash cart and defibrillator"). | 1 | Rapid activation of emergency medical response. |
| 4 | Simultaneously assessed breathing (looked for chest rise) and carotid pulse (groove between trachea and sternocleidomastoid) for at least 5 but no more than 10 seconds. | 2 | Confirms cardiac arrest without delaying compressions. |
| 5 | If no pulse and no normal breathing (or only gasping): Immediately placed heels of hands on lower half of sternum (center of chest). | 2 | Correct anatomical landmark prevents rib fractures and maximizes cardiac output. |
| 6 | Positioned shoulders directly over hands with arms straight and elbows locked. | 1 | Uses upper body weight for effective compressions. |
| 7 | Delivered 30 high-quality chest compressions: Depth of 5 to 6 cm (2 to 2.4 inches), Rate of 100 to 120 compressions per minute. | 3 | Generates critical coronary and cerebral perfusion pressure. |
| 8 | Allowed complete chest recoil after each compression without leaning on the chest. | 2 | Allows ventricles to refill with blood between compressions. |
| 9 | Opened airway using Head-Tilt Chin-Lift maneuver (or Jaw-Thrust if cervical trauma suspected). | 1 | Lifts tongue away from posterior pharyngeal wall. |
| 10 | Delivered 2 rescue breaths using Bag-Valve-Mask (EC-clamp technique: E formed by 3 fingers on mandible, C formed by thumb and index on mask) over 1 second each, observing visible chest rise. | 2 | Adequate oxygenation while minimizing gastric inflation. |
| 11 | Maintained compression-to-ventilation ratio of 30:2 with minimal interruption (< 10 seconds). | 2 | Prevents drop in coronary perfusion. |
| 12 | As soon as AED arrived: Turned ON AED, attached pads correctly (Right anterolateral upper chest below clavicle, Left mid-axillary line below nipple). | 1 | Early defibrillation is key in shockable rhythms. |
| 13 | Cleared area during heart rhythm analysis ("Stand clear!"); delivered shock if advised after ensuring everyone is clear; resumed CPR immediately starting with compressions. | 1 | Resuming compressions immediately minimizes post-shock downtime. |

### Procedure AHN-02: Standard 12-Lead Electrocardiogram (ECG) Recording
*Time Allotted: 5 Minutes | Maximum Score: 10 Marks*

| Step # | Procedure Steps | Max | Criteria & Scientific Rationale |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| 1 | Checked patient identity, explained procedure, addressed concerns regarding electrical shock, obtained consent. | 1 | Good bedside practice; allays anxiety. |
| 2 | Exposed chest, wrists, and ankles; ensured patient is relaxed, supine, warm, and limbs not touching metal bed frame. | 1 | Prevents somatic muscle tremors and AC interference. |
| 3 | Cleaned skin with alcohol swab; applied conductive gel (if using bulb electrodes) or peeled disposable electrodes. | 1 | Decreases skin impedance for clear signal. |
| 4 | Connected 4 Limb leads correctly: Red (Right Arm), Yellow (Left Arm), Green (Left Leg), Black (Right Leg / Earth). | 2 | Standard Indian/European color code. 🚩 **Red Flag: Lead transposition** |
| 5 | Correctly placed V1 & V2: V1 in 4th Intercostal Space (ICS) right sternal border; V2 in 4th ICS left sternal border. | 1 | Correct anatomical localization. |
| 6 | Placed V4 in 5th ICS midclavicular line; placed V3 midway between V2 and V4. | 1 | Standard precordial orientation. |
| 7 | Placed V5 in 5th ICS anterior axillary line; placed V6 in 5th ICS midaxillary line (horizontal level with V4). | 1 | Accurate lateral wall representation. |
| 8 | Verified machine settings (Paper speed: 25 mm/sec, Voltage calibration: 10 mm/mV); instructed patient to remain still and breathe quietly. | 1 | Standard diagnostic calibration. |
| 9 | Recorded ECG strip; inspected tracing for lead reversal, baseline drift, or artifact interference. | 1 | Quality control before disconnecting patient. |
| 10 | Disconnected leads, wiped gel from patient's chest; documented patient name, age, date, time, and clinical indication on tracing. | 1 | Clinical safety and legal record. |

### Procedure CHN-01: Neonatal Resuscitation Protocol (NRP / NSSK)
*Time Allotted: 5 Minutes | Maximum Score: 20 Marks*

```
STATION SETUP:
- Neonatal resuscitation manikin under preheated Radiant Warmer
- Warm dry linen (2 towels), shoulder roll (towel), bulb syringe, suction catheter (8/10 Fr)
- Neonatal Bag-Valve-Mask (250-500 mL) with term/preterm masks
- Stethoscope, pulse oximeter with neonatal probe, timer/clock
- Normal saline, 1 mL syringe, epinephrine ampoule (simulator)
```

| Step # | Procedure Steps | Max | Criteria & Scientific Rationale |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| 1 | Asked 3 rapid questions at birth: "Term gestation? Good muscle tone? Breathing or crying?". | 1 | Determines if baby needs resuscitation or routine care. |
| 2 | If NO to any: Immediately placed newborn under preheated Radiant Warmer; removed wet linen. | 1 | Prevents hypothermia and cold stress. |
| 3 | Positioned head in neutral or slight extension ("sniffing position") using a small shoulder roll under neck/shoulders. | 2 | Aligns pharyngeal, laryngeal, and tracheal axes. |
| 4 | Cleared secretions: Suctioned Mouth first, then Nose (M before N) using bulb syringe or suction catheter (< 100 mmHg). | 2 | Suctioning nose first can induce gasping and aspiration of mouth fluids. |
| 5 | Dried newborn thoroughly; provided tactile stimulation by rubbing back or flicking soles of feet (max 2 times). | 1 | Initiates breathing reflex; replaces wet linen. |
| 6 | Assessed breathing and heart rate (auscultated apical pulse with stethoscope for 6 seconds $\times 10$). | 2 | Rapid evaluation within Golden Minute. |
| 7 | If apneic, gasping, or Heart Rate < 100 bpm: Initiated Positive Pressure Ventilation (PPV) immediately using neonatal self-inflating bag. | 3 | Hypoxia is primary cause of neonatal bradycardia; ventilation is key. |
| 8 | Applied correct size mask covering chin, mouth, and nose (avoiding eyes); achieved tight airtight seal with C-grip. | 2 | Prevents air leak; avoids ocular trauma. |
| 9 | Ventilated at rate of 40 to 60 breaths per minute ("Breathe, two, three, Breathe, two, three") with adequate pressure to produce visible chest rise. | 2 | Matches physiological neonatal respiratory rate. |
| 10 | Attached pulse oximeter sensor to Right Hand/Wrist (Pre-ductal saturation). | 1 | Measures accurate cerebral/coronary oxygenation. |
| 11 | Reassessed heart rate after 30 seconds of effective PPV with chest rise. | 1 | Clinical decision milestone. |
| 12 | If Heart Rate remains < 60 bpm despite 30 seconds of effective ventilation: Commenced chest compressions (2-thumb encircling-hands technique on lower third of sternum). | 1 | Optimal compression mechanics for neonates. |
| 13 | Coordinated compressions and ventilations in 3:1 ratio (90 compressions and 30 breaths = 120 events per minute). | 1 | Standard NRP resuscitation cycle. |

| 7 | Plotted measurements on WHO Gender-Specific Growth Chart (Weight-for-Age, Length-for-Age, Weight-for-Length). | 1 | Diagnostic growth curve classification. |

### Procedure OBG-01: Antenatal Abdominal Palpation (Obstetrical Grips / Leopold's Maneuvers)
*Time Allotted: 5 Minutes | Maximum Score: 20 Marks*

```
STATION SETUP:
- Antenatal examination manikin with pregnant abdomen and palpation fetus
- Examination table with clean sheet, drape sheet, measuring tape
- Pinard fetal stethoscope and handheld Doppler with acoustic gel
- Hand hygiene facility / sanitizer
```

| Step # | Procedure Steps | Max | Criteria & Scientific Rationale |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| 1 | Checked antenatal records; verified gestational age (LMP, EDD); explained procedure; ensured woman emptied bladder prior to exam. | 1 | Full bladder elevates uterus and causes discomfort. |
| 2 | Maintained privacy; draped mother exposing abdomen from xiphoid sternum to symphysis pubis; placed her in dorsal position with knees slightly flexed. | 1 | Relaxes abdominal wall muscles. |
| 3 | Performed hand hygiene; rubbed hands together to warm them before touching mother's abdomen. | 1 | Cold hands trigger abdominal muscle contraction and maternal discomfort. |
| 4 | Inspected abdomen: Noted shape, contour, surgical scars, striae gravidarum, and linea nigra. | 1 | Baseline obstetric inspection. |
| 5 | Measured Fundal Height: Placed ulnar border of left hand at fundus; measured with tape from upper border of symphysis pubis to fundus along midline with centimeters facing down (blind measurement). | 2 | Prevents examiner bias; assesses fetal growth against gestational age. |
| 6 | **First Maneuver (Fundal Grip)**: Stood facing woman's face; palpated fundus with both palms. Differentiated breech (soft, broad, irregular, non-ballotable) from head (hard, round, smooth, ballotable). | 3 | Determines which fetal pole occupies uterine fundus. |
| 7 | **Second Maneuver (Lateral / Umbilical Grip)**: Placed palms on both lateral sides of uterus. Held one side steady while other hand palpated for smooth, continuous convex resistance (fetal back) vs small irregular knob-like projections (fetal extremities). | 3 | Determines fetal lie, position, and location of fetal back for FHS auscultation. |
| 8 | **Third Maneuver (Pawlik's Grip)**: Grasped lower pole of uterus just above symphysis pubis between thumb and four fingers of right hand. Assessed if presenting part is engaged (fixed) or non-engaged (movable/ballotable). | 3 | Determines presenting part and engagement. |
| 9 | **Fourth Maneuver (Pelvic Grip)**: Turned and stood facing woman's feet. Placed tips of first three fingers of both hands on lower abdomen and pressed downward into pelvic brim. Assessed convergence/divergence of fingers (attitude and depth of engagement). | 2 | Verifies degree of fetal head descent and flexion. |
| 10 | **Fetal Heart Sound (FHS) Auscultation**: Placed Pinard stethoscope or Doppler on maternal abdomen over convex fetal back; palpated maternal radial pulse simultaneously. | 2 | Ensures sound heard is fetal heart rate (110-160 bpm) and not maternal arterial pulse. |
| 11 | Counted FHS for one full minute; noted rhythm and rate. | 1 | Detects fetal well-being or fetal distress. |
| 12 | Helped mother sit up comfortably; documented fundal height, lie, presentation, position, engagement, and FHR; explained findings reassuringly to mother. | 1 | Communication and documentation. |

### Procedure OBG-02: Active Management of Third Stage of Labour (AMTSL)
*Time Allotted: 5 Minutes | Maximum Score: 20 Marks*

```
STATION SETUP:
- Birthing simulator with delivery canal, placenta with membranes and umbilical cord
- Delivery tray: Cord clamps (2), cord cutting scissors, sponge forceps, sterile gauze
- Oxytocin 10 IU ampoule, 2 mL syringe, 23G needle, kidney tray
- Delivery drape, sterile gloves, BMW bins (Yellow, Red, Blue, White)
```

| Step # | Procedure Steps | Max | Criteria & Scientific Rationale |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| 1 | Immediately upon delivery of the baby: Palpated maternal abdomen to rule out presence of an undiagnosed second twin. | 2 | Administration of uterotonic with an undiagnosed twin inside causes fetal asphyxia and uterine rupture. 🚩 **Red Flag** |
| 2 | Within 1 minute of childbirth: Administered **10 IU Oxytocin Intramuscularly (IM)** into anterior thigh (Vastus Lateralis). | 3 | Evidence-based gold standard for preventing Postpartum Hemorrhage (PPH). |
| 3 | Delayed cord clamping for 1 to 3 minutes (or until cord pulsations cease) while placing baby on mother's chest for skin-to-skin contact. | 2 | Increases neonatal iron stores and promotes physiological transition. |
| 4 | Applied first cord clamp 2 cm from neonatal umbilicus; applied second clamp 3 cm further; cut cord cleanly with sterile scissors between clamps. | 1 | Sterile cord transection. |
| 5 | Observed for clinical signs of placental separation (lengthening of cord, sudden gush of blood, uterus becomes globular and firm). | 2 | Prevents traction on unseparated placenta (prevents uterine inversion). |
| 6 | Performed **Controlled Cord Traction (CCT / Brandt-Andrews Technique)**: Placed one hand above symphysis pubis applying counter-traction upwards; applied gentle, steady downward traction on clamped cord during uterine contraction. | 4 | Safely delivers placenta while counter-pressure prevents uterine inversion. 🚩 **Red Flag: Pulling cord without counter-traction** |
| 7 | As placenta emerged at introitus: Received placenta in cupped hands and twisted it slowly in a circular motion to form membranes into a rope. | 2 | Prevents tearing and retention of fetal membranes. |
| 8 | Immediately performed vigorous **Uterine Massage** through abdominal wall until uterus became rock-hard and well-contracted. | 2 | Stimulates physiological myometrial retraction and seals bleeding sinusoids. |
