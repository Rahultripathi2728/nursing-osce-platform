### Step-by-Step Scoring Rubric:

| Step # | Evaluation Step | Max | Score [0 / 1 / 2] | Examiner Rationale & Critical Points |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| 1 | Introduced self professionally, verified patient name, stated interview purpose, assured confidentiality. | 1 | | Therapeutic rapport. |
| 2 | Evaluated **General Appearance & Behavior**: Grooming, hygiene, dress appropriateness, posture, eye contact, facial expression, and psychomotor activity. | 3 | | Observational baseline. |
| 3 | Evaluated **Speech**: Rate, quantity, volume, tone, spontaneity, coherence, and reaction time. | 2 | | Reflects thought flow and affective tone. |
| 4 | Assessed **Mood & Affect**: Inquired about subjective mood ("How have you been feeling inside?"); observed objective affect (congruence, range, blunting). | 2 | | Emotional reactivity. |
| 5 | Assessed **Thought Process / Form**: Evaluated continuity and progression of thought (flight of ideas, loosening of associations, circumstantiality, blocking). | 2 | | Formal thought disorders. |
| 6 | Assessed **Thought Content**: Systematically screened for delusions (persecutory, grandiose, reference), obsessions, phobias, and overvalued ideas. | 2 | | Psychotic cognitive content. |
| 7 | **Assessed Suicidal & Homicidal Ideation**: Asked sensitive, direct questions regarding hopelessness, self-harm, or suicidal intent and plans. | 2 | | 🚩 **Critical Safety: Must assess suicide risk.** |
| 8 | Evaluated **Perception**: Screened for hallucinations (Auditory, Visual, Tactile) and illusions ("Do you hear voices when nobody is around?"). | 2 | | Sensory perceptual disturbances. |
| 9 | Assessed **Cognitive Functions**: Evaluated orientation (Time, Place, Person), Attention & Concentration (Serial 7s), and Memory (Immediate, Recent, Remote). | 2 | | Organic brain impairment screening. |
| 10 | Assessed **Insight & Judgment**: Tested social judgment and inquired about patient's awareness of illness (Insight Grades 1 to 6). | 2 | | Prognosis and compliance. |
