### Step-by-Step Scoring Rubric:

| Step # | Evaluation Step | Max | Score [0 / 1 / 2] | Examiner Rationale & Critical Points |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| 1 | Verified physician order; checked 2 patient identifiers; explained procedure; ensured strict privacy with screens; obtained consent. | 1 | | Patient rights and clinical safety. |
| 2 | Performed medical hand hygiene; positioned patient in dorsal recumbent position with knees flexed and hips abducted; placed waterproof sheet. | 1 | | Optimal exposure and bed protection. |
| 3 | Performed preliminary perineal wash with soap and water if soiled; discarded unsterile gloves; performed hand wash. | 1 | | Reduces bacterial inoculum around introitus. |
| 4 | Opened sterile catheterization tray maintaining strict sterile perimeter; created sterile working field. | 1 | | Aseptic field preparation. |
| 5 | **Donned sterile surgical gloves** without touching outer sterile surfaces. | 2 | | 🚩 **Red Flag: Breaching sterile glove technique.** |
| 6 | Tested Foley balloon with sterile water; lubricated catheter tip (2 inches) with sterile water-soluble jelly. | 1 | | Verifies balloon integrity and eases insertion. |
| 7 | **Using non-dominant hand, gently retracted labia minora** to expose urethral meatus clearly. Maintained hand in position until catheter was placed. | 2 | | Non-dominant hand is now contaminated; cannot be released. |
| 8 | **Using dominant sterile hand and forceps**, swabbed meatus with antiseptic-soaked cotton balls from top to bottom (single swab per stroke). | 2 | | Aseptic meatal preparation. |
| 9 | Held lubricated catheter 2-3 inches from tip with sterile dominant hand; gently introduced into meatus until urine flowed. | 2 | | Confirms entry into bladder lumen. |
| 10 | **Advanced catheter an additional 1 to 2 inches** after initial urine return before inflating retention balloon. | 2 | | Guarantees balloon is inside bladder, avoiding urethral trauma. |
| 11 | Inflated balloon slowly with 10 mL sterile water; gently pulled back until snug resistance was felt at bladder neck. | 2 | | Prevents inadvertent catheter dislodgement. |
| 12 | Connected catheter to sterile drainage bag using aseptic connection technique. | 1 | | Maintains closed drainage system. |
| 13 | Secured catheter tubing to patient's inner thigh with slack; hung drainage bag below bladder level on bed frame (not on floor or side rails). | 1 | | Prevents urine reflux and accidental pulling. |
| 14 | Disposed contaminated items in Yellow BMW bin; removed gloves; performed hand hygiene; recorded catheter size, balloon volume, urine character, and time. | 1 | | Waste management and clinical documentation. |
