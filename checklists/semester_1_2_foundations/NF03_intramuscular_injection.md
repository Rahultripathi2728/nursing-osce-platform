# OSCE Checklist: Adult Intramuscular (IM) Injection (Ventrogluteal Site)
**Course:** Nursing Foundation II / Adult Health Nursing I | **Semesters:** II / III  
**Station Type:** Manned (Observer Station) | **Time Allotted:** 5 Minutes | **Max Marks:** 20

### Station Setup & Equipment:
1. Manikin: Injection trainer or Ventrogluteal anatomical model.
2. Syringes: 2 mL / 3 mL with 21G, 22G, 23G (1.5 inch) needles.
3. Consumables: Medication ampoule (simulated), 70% isopropyl alcohol swabs, sterile dry cotton swabs, gauze.
4. Tray: Kidney tray, clean gloves, prescription sheet / Medication Administration Record (MAR).
5. BMW Bins: White puncture-proof sharps box, Yellow bin for infected cotton.

### Step-by-Step Scoring Rubric:

| Step # | Evaluation Step | Max | Score [0 / 1 / 2] | Examiner Rationale & Critical Points |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Phase 1: Pre-Procedure & Preparation** | | | | |
| 1 | Verified doctor's prescription order; cross-checked the 6 Rights of medication administration (Right Patient, Drug, Dose, Route, Time, Documentation). | 2 | | Legal & patient safety standard. |
| 2 | Identified patient using two independent identifiers (Name and Hospital ID/Wristband); inquired about allergy history. | 1 | | Positive identification prevents wrong-patient errors. |
| 3 | Explained medication indication, action, and expected sensation to patient; obtained informed verbal consent. | 1 | | Relieves anxiety and promotes cooperation. |
| 4 | Performed WHO 6-step hand hygiene; donned clean disposable gloves. | 2 | | Infection control standard. |
| 5 | Assembled equipment ergonomically on kidney tray; inspected ampoule for clarity, sediment, and expiry date. | 1 | | Equipment readiness and drug verification. |
| **Phase 2: Procedure Execution** | | | | |
| 6 | Snapped ampoule neck safely away from body using sterile gauze piece. | 1 | | Prevents cut injury and glass splinter contamination. |
| 7 | Aspirated prescribed volume without touching plunger or ampoule rim; expelled air bubbles at eye level. | 2 | | Accurate dosage measurement. |
| 8 | Changed drawing needle to a fresh sterile intramuscular needle (21G or 22G, 1.5 inch). | 1 | | Minimizes pain and tissue trauma. |
| 9 | Positioned patient in side-lying (Sims) position with upper leg flexed to relax gluteal muscles. | 1 | | Relaxes muscle belly for painless entry. |
| 10 | **Accurately located Ventrogluteal landmark**: Placed palm on greater trochanter, index finger on anterior superior iliac spine (ASIS), middle finger extended toward iliac crest forming a 'V'. Injection site is the exact center of 'V'. | 2 | | **Key Competency**: Avoids sciatic nerve and gluteal vessels. |
| 11 | Disinfected site with 70% alcohol swab using outward spiral motion (2 inches diameter); allowed 30s to dry. | 1 | | Wet alcohol causes stinging and incomplete antisepsis. |
| 12 | Spread skin taut (or performed Z-track); inserted needle smoothly at 90° angle with quick dart-like motion. | 2 | | Ensures true intramuscular deposition. |
| 13 | Aspirated plunger for 5-10 seconds to confirm absence of blood return. | 1 | | Confirms needle is not in a blood vessel. |
| 14 | Injected medication smoothly at a steady rate of ~10 seconds per mL; waited 5s before withdrawal. | 1 | | Allows medication to disperse in muscle tissue. |
| 15 | Withdrew needle smoothly at 90° while applying gentle pressure with sterile dry cotton (did NOT massage). | 1 | | Prevents tissue damage and hematoma. |
| **Phase 3: Post-Procedure & Waste Disposal** | | | | |
| 16 | Discarded uncapped needle and syringe directly into White puncture-proof container; disposed swab into Yellow BMW bin. | 1 | | Prevents needle-stick injuries (BMW Rules 2016). |
| 17 | Repositioned patient comfortably; removed gloves; performed hand hygiene. | 1 | | Patient comfort and infection prevention. |
| 18 | Accurately recorded drug, dosage, route, site, time, and signed the MAR. | 1 | | Complete clinical and legal documentation. |
