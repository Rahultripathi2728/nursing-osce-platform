### Step-by-Step Scoring Rubric:

| Step # | Evaluation Step | Max | Score [0 / 1 / 2] | Examiner Rationale & Critical Points |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| 1 | Asked initial 3 questions at birth: "Term gestation? Good muscle tone? Breathing or crying?". | 1 | | Determines need for resuscitation. |
| 2 | If NO to any: Placed newborn under preheated radiant warmer; positioned under heat lamp. | 1 | | Prevents hypothermia and cold stress. |
| 3 | Positioned head in neutral or slight sniffing position using a small shoulder roll. | 2 | | Aligns pharyngeal and tracheal axes. |
| 4 | **Cleared secretions**: Suctioned **Mouth first, then Nose (M before N)** using bulb syringe or suction catheter (< 100 mmHg). | 2 | | Suctioning nose first triggers gasp and fluid aspiration. |
| 5 | Dried baby thoroughly with warm towel; discarded wet linen; gently stimulated soles or back (max 2 times). | 2 | | Sensory trigger for spontaneous respiration. |
| 6 | Assessed breathing and heart rate (auscultated apical pulse with stethoscope for 6 seconds $\times 10$). | 2 | | Clinical status check within Golden Minute. |
| 7 | If apneic, gasping, or HR < 100 bpm: Initiated **Positive Pressure Ventilation (PPV)** immediately using neonatal self-inflating bag. | 3 | | 🚩 **Critical Step: Ventilation is primary treatment for neonatal bradycardia.** |
| 8 | Applied correct mask size covering mouth, nose, and tip of chin (not eyes); formed airtight seal with C-clamp grip. | 2 | | Prevents air leaks and ocular compression. |
| 9 | Ventilated at rate of **40 to 60 breaths per minute** ("Breathe, two, three...") ensuring visible chest rise. | 2 | | Matches physiological neonatal breathing frequency. |
| 10 | Attached pulse oximeter probe to Right Hand / Wrist (Pre-ductal saturation). | 1 | | Pre-ductal blood reflects cerebral oxygenation. |
| 11 | Reassessed HR after 30 seconds of effective PPV with chest rise. | 1 | | Informs progression to compressions or weaning. |
| 12 | If HR remains < 60 bpm: Commenced chest compressions (2-thumb encircling hands on lower third of sternum) coordinated with ventilation at **3:1 ratio** (90 compressions : 30 breaths/min). | 1 | | Restores cardiac output in severe bradycardia. |
