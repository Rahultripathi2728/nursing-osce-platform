**Course:** Community Health Nursing I & II | **Semesters:** V / VII  
**Station Type:** Manned (Active Skill Station) | **Time Allotted:** 7 Minutes | **Max Marks:** 20

### Step-by-Step Scoring Rubric:

| Step # | Evaluation Step | Max | Score [0 / 1 / 2] | Examiner Rationale & Critical Points |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| 1 | Greeted family politely, introduced self, explained purpose of home visit, obtained verbal consent. | 1 | | Community rapport and etiquette. |
| 2 | Selected a clean, dry, flat surface away from children, animals, and open kitchen hearth. | 1 | | Prevents environmental contamination of bag. |
| 3 | Spread clean sheet (plastic or newspaper) on surface with clean side facing up. | 1 | | Establishes a designated clean field. |
| 4 | Placed community bag on sheet with locks/buckles facing toward the nurse. | 1 | | Ergonomic layout. |
| 5 | Opened outer compartment of bag without touching inner sterile equipment; removed soap dish, towel, and plastic apron. | 2 | | Strict boundary between clean and contaminated areas. |
| 6 | Put on clean plastic apron; washed hands thoroughly using soap and clean water (WHO steps); dried with clean towel. | 2 | | Prevents transmitting pathogens from community into family home. |
| 7 | Opened inner compartment of bag; retrieved only the required equipment onto a kidney tray on the sheet. | 2 | | Prevents contaminating unused inner items. |
| 8 | **Closed bag flap immediately** after removing needed items. | 1 | | Protects bag interior from flies, dust, and aerosols. |
| 9 | Performed clinical procedure / family health assessment with technical competence and empathy. | 4 | | Core clinical delivery. |
| 10 | Cleaned and disinfected all used articles (wiped thermometer with spirit swab from bulb to stem; sanitized instruments). | 2 | | Decontamination before repacking. |
| 11 | Washed and dried hands thoroughly after cleaning equipment. | 1 | | Infection control prior to bag closure. |
| 12 | Returned clean articles to inner bag; removed apron (folded clean side out) and packed in outer pocket; folded sheet (dirty side inward) into outer pouch; locked bag. | 2 | | Complete containment and zero cross-contamination. |
