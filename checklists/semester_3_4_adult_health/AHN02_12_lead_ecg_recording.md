# OSCE Checklist: Standard 12-Lead Electrocardiogram (ECG) Recording
**Course:** Adult Health Nursing I & II | **Semesters:** III / IV  
**Station Type:** Manned (Observer Station) | **Time Allotted:** 5 Minutes | **Max Marks:** 20

### Step-by-Step Scoring Rubric:

| Step # | Evaluation Step | Max | Score [0 / 1 / 2] | Examiner Rationale & Critical Points |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| 1 | Checked physician order and patient identity; explained procedure (reassured patient that no electricity enters the body); obtained consent. | 2 | | Relieves anxiety and avoids muscle tremor artifacts. |
| 2 | Exposed chest, wrists, and ankles; ensured warm environment; positioned patient supine and relaxed with limbs uncrossed. | 1 | | Prevents somatic tremor artifact. |
| 3 | Performed hand hygiene; cleaned electrode sites with alcohol swab if skin oily or sweaty; allowed to dry. | 1 | | Reduces skin electrical impedance. |
| 4 | **Connected 4 Limb Leads accurately**: Red (Right Arm), Yellow (Left Arm), Green (Left Leg), Black (Right Leg / Neutral Ground). | 3 | | 🚩 **Critical: Lead transposition produces pseudo-infarction patterns.** |
| 5 | Located and placed **V1 & V2**: V1 in 4th Intercostal Space (ICS) right sternal border; V2 in 4th ICS left sternal border. | 3 | | Identifies 4th intercostal space accurately. |
| 6 | Located and placed **V4**: 5th Intercostal Space in Left Mid-Clavicular Line. | 2 | | Reference anchor for precordial placement. |
| 7 | Placed **V3**: Midway on a diagonal line between V2 and V4. | 1 | | Intermediate electrode position. |
| 8 | Located and placed **V5 & V6**: V5 in 5th ICS Left Anterior Axillary Line; V6 in 5th ICS Left Mid-Axillary Line (horizontal to V4). | 2 | | Lateral wall view of the left ventricle. |
| 9 | Checked ECG machine calibration settings: Paper speed at **25 mm/s**, Voltage calibration at **10 mm/mV**. | 2 | | Diagnostic accuracy standard. |
| 10 | Instructed patient to remain completely still and breathe quietly; printed tracing; inspected for baseline wander or artifact. | 2 | | Quality assurance before disconnecting patient. |
| 11 | Disconnected leads, wiped gel, repositioned patient, labeled ECG strip with Name, Age, MRN, Date, Time, and clinical indication. | 1 | | Complete clinical record. |
