### Step-by-Step Scoring Rubric:

| Step # | Evaluation Step | Max | Score [0 / 1 / 2] | Examiner Rationale & Critical Points |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| 1 | Assessed scene safety (verified environment is safe for rescuer and victim). | 1 | | Rescuer safety first. |
| 2 | Checked responsiveness: Tapped shoulders firmly and asked loudly, "Are you okay?". | 1 | | Verifies true unresponsiveness. |
| 3 | Shouted for immediate help: "Call hospital Code Blue / 108; bring emergency crash cart and defibrillator!". | 1 | | Activates emergency medical services team. |
| 4 | Simultaneously assessed breathing (looked for chest rise) and carotid pulse for 5 to 10 seconds. | 2 | | Confirms cardiac arrest without delaying compressions. |
| 5 | **Landmark & Position**: Placed heel of dominant hand on center of chest (lower half of sternum); placed other hand on top with fingers interlaced; positioned shoulders vertically over hands with straight locked elbows. | 2 | | Anatomical precision delivers force through sternum. |
| 6 | **High-Quality Chest Compressions**: Delivered 30 compressions at a depth of 5 to 6 cm (2 to 2.4 inches) and a rate of 100 to 120 compressions/minute. | 3 | | 🚩 **Critical: Inadequate depth or rate severely impairs perfusion.** |
| 7 | Allowed complete chest recoil after each compression without leaning on the chest. | 2 | | Ensures cardiac chambers refill between beats. |
| 8 | Opened airway using Head-Tilt Chin-Lift maneuver (or Jaw-Thrust if spinal injury suspected). | 1 | | Clears tongue from posterior pharynx. |
| 9 | Delivered 2 rescue breaths with Bag-Valve-Mask (EC-clamp seal) over 1 second each, observing visible chest rise. | 2 | | Oxygenates without causing gastric inflation. |
| 10 | Maintained 30:2 ratio with minimal interruptions (< 10 seconds between compression cycles). | 2 | | Minimizes coronary perfusion drops. |
| 11 | **AED Operation**: Turned ON AED immediately upon arrival; attached pads correctly (Right upper chest below clavicle; Left mid-axillary line below nipple). | 2 | | Early defibrillation saves lives. |
| 12 | Cleared victim during rhythm analysis; cleared victim and pressed shock button when indicated; resumed chest compressions immediately after shock. | 1 | | Safety during shock; eliminates post-shock delay. |
