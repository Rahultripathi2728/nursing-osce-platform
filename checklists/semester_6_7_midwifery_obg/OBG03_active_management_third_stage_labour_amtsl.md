### Step-by-Step Scoring Rubric:

| Step # | Evaluation Step | Max | Score [0 / 1 / 2] | Examiner Rationale & Critical Points |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| 1 | Immediately after delivery of the infant: **Palpated maternal abdomen to rule out presence of an undiagnosed second twin**. | 3 | | 🚩 **Critical: Giving uterotonic with an undiagnosed twin inside causes fatal fetal hypoxia & uterine rupture.** |
| 2 | **Administered 10 IU Oxytocin IM** in maternal anterior thigh (Vastus Lateralis) within 1 minute of birth. | 3 | | Gold standard uterotonic for preventing primary PPH. |
| 3 | Delayed cord clamping for 1-3 minutes while placing baby skin-to-skin on mother's chest, then clamped and cut cord sterilely. | 2 | | Physiological transition and neonatal iron stores. |
| 4 | Watched for signs of placental separation (lengthening of cord, small gush of blood, uterus globular & firm). | 2 | | Prevents pulling on an unseparated placenta. |
| 5 | **Delivered placenta by Controlled Cord Traction (CCT / Brandt-Andrews)**: Placed palm above pubic symphysis applying firm counter-traction upwards; applied gentle, steady downward traction on clamped cord during uterine contraction. | 4 | | 🚩 **Critical: Applying cord traction without counter-traction causes Uterine Inversion.** |
| 6 | As placenta emerged: Received placenta in cupped palms and rotated in gentle circles to twist membranes into a rope. | 2 | | Prevents tearing and retention of fetal membranes. |
| 7 | **Immediately performed vigorous Uterine Massage** through abdominal wall until uterus was rock-hard and contracted. | 2 | | Stimulates myometrial retraction and seals open sinusoids. |
| 8 | Inspected maternal and fetal surfaces of placenta for missing cotyledons, succenturiate lobes, or torn vessels; checked maternal perineum for tears. | 2 | | Excludes retained tissue and traumatic bleeding. |
