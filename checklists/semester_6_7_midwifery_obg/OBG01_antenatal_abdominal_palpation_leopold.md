### Step-by-Step Scoring Rubric:

| Step # | Evaluation Step | Max | Score [0 / 1 / 2] | Examiner Rationale & Critical Points |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| 1 | Checked antenatal record (LMP, EDD, parity); explained procedure; ensured woman emptied bladder before examination. | 1 | | Distended bladder elevates uterus and causes pain. |
| 2 | Maintained privacy; draped mother exposing abdomen from xiphisternum to pubic symphysis; positioned supine with knees slightly bent. | 1 | | Relaxes abdominal rectus muscles. |
| 3 | Performed hand hygiene; rubbed hands together to warm palms before touching mother's abdomen. | 1 | | Cold hands stimulate abdominal wall contraction. |
| 4 | Inspected abdomen for shape, contour, scars, linea nigra, and striae gravidarum. | 1 | | Baseline inspection. |
| 5 | **Measured Fundal Height**: Placed ulnar border of left hand at fundus; measured from superior border of pubic symphysis to fundus using tape reversed (centimeters facing down). | 2 | | Blind measurement eliminates examiner bias. |
| 6 | **First Maneuver (Fundal Grip)**: Stood facing woman's face; gently palpated fundal pole with both palms. Differentiated breech (soft, broad, irregular) from head (hard, round, ballotable). | 3 | | Determines which fetal pole lies at uterine fundus. |
| 7 | **Second Maneuver (Lateral / Umbilical Grip)**: Placed palms on lateral uterine walls. Stabilized one side while palpating other for smooth continuous curve (fetal back) vs small irregular knob-like nodules (fetal limbs). | 3 | | Determines fetal lie and location of back for FHS auscultation. |
| 8 | **Third Maneuver (Pawlik's Grip)**: Grasped lower uterine pole just above pubic symphysis between thumb and fingers of right hand. Assessed if presenting part is ballotable (free) or fixed (engaged). | 3 | | Determines presenting part and engagement. |
| 9 | **Fourth Maneuver (Pelvic Grip)**: Turned facing mother's feet; pressed fingers of both hands downward toward pelvic inlet. Assessed convergence/divergence of fingers (attitude and flexion of head). | 2 | | Confirms degree of descent into pelvic brim. |
| 10 | **Fetal Heart Sound (FHS) Auscultation**: Placed Pinard stethoscope or Doppler over convex fetal back; palpated maternal radial pulse simultaneously to differentiate maternal pulse from fetal heart rate (110-160 bpm). | 2 | | 🚩 **Critical: Must differentiate maternal arterial pulse from fetal heart sounds.** |
| 11 | Assisted mother to sit up comfortably; communicated findings reassuringly; documented fundal height, lie, presentation, position, and FHR. | 1 | | Communication and clinical record. |
